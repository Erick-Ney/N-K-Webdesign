Our story

We know what a bad website costs you.

N&K Webdesign was founded in Munich out of a simple frustration: watching excellent local businesses lose customers every day because their websites looked outdated, loaded slowly, or — worse — didn't exist at all.

We combined a background in design with engineering-grade development standards to offer something the market was missing: websites that look world-class and actually perform.

Every project we take on starts with one question: what does success look like for your business? Not how many pages you need, not what your competitor's site looks like — what results will make this project worth it for you? That drives every decision we make.

Since 2022, we've worked with businesses across Munich and Bavaria — from sole traders getting online for the first time to established companies that needed a complete digital rebrand.

What we believe

How we think
about web design.

Results first

Design is a tool, not a goal. Every visual decision must serve your business objectives — conversions, trust, or enquiries. Pretty is a bonus.

Speed & reliability

A slow website loses customers. We build for performance from the first line of code — not as an afterthought. Sub-2-second loads are our baseline.

Honesty over sales

We won't recommend features you don't need. Fixed pricing, transparent processes, and honest timelines — before any work begins.

Long-term partnership

We don't disappear after launch. Most clients work with us for years — on updates, SEO, or new campaigns. We build relationships, not transactions.
