What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners about how visitors use their site.

How we use cookies

We use three categories of cookies on this website:

1. Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are set in response to actions you make, such as remembering your cookie preferences. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information.

Cookie name Provider Purpose Duration Type
cookieConsent nk-webdesign.de Stores your cookie consent preferences 1 year localStorage
theme nk-webdesign.de Stores your dark/light mode preference Persistent localStorage

2. Analytics cookies (optional)

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website. All data is anonymised — IP addresses are truncated before storage and no personally identifiable information is collected.

Cookie name Provider Purpose Duration Type
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors 2 years Cookie
_ga_* Google Analytics Persists session state 2 years Cookie
_gid Google Analytics Distinguishes users (session) 24 hours Cookie

3. Marketing cookies (optional)

Marketing cookies may be used in future to show relevant advertising on other websites. They are only set with your explicit consent. Currently no marketing cookies are active on this site.

How to delete cookies in your browser

You can also delete cookies directly in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear browsing data → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search and services → Clear browsing data

Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of this website.
